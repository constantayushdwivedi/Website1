Common questions

Do you teach school students?

Yes. We coach students from Class 3 to Class 12, with sessions after school hours and on weekends. Parents get regular progress updates and are welcome to join any session.

Do I need prior coding experience?

No. We start from where you are, whether that is your first block-coding game, your first Python program or a nearly finished degree.

How often will I meet my mentor?

Most students meet once a week for 60 minutes, with messaging support between sessions.

Are sessions online?

Yes. Sessions run on video call, so you can join from home, school or campus.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on the program and session count. Book a free intro call for clear options.
